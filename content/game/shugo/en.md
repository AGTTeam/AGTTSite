## About the game
Shugo Chara! Norinori! Chara-nari Rhythm♪ is the last of three Nintendo DS games based on the Shugo Chara license. Unlike the first two games, which were primarily dating sims, Chara-nari Rhythm is a rhythm-action game.

The story is set after episodes 85-86 of the anime, and features the return of Yua and Cecil, who have been called in to help heroine Amu Hinamori face off against a new unknown threat.
