## Notes
**Known Issues:**
The anime cutscenes have intermittent screen tearing. This also happens with the original game, and is less noticeable on hardware.

**Secret Codes:**
This game contains a menu where secret codes can be entered to unlock bonus content. The original Japanese codes will not work with the patched version, so we’ve hidden the new translated codes across our socials. (The full list is also available in the patch readme.) Happy hunting!
