## About the game
Successors to L is the second of three Nintendo DS games based on the Death Note license. It covers the series’ storyline in its entirety, from Light finding the notebook to the Yellow Box Warehouse.

The gameplay is inspired by the board game "Scotland Yard", with the rules and goals of each round changing depending on whether the player is controlling Kira or the investigators.
