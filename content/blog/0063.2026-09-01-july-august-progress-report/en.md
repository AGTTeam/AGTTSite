## \[GBC\] Hunter x Hunter: Forbidden Treasure
![Hunter x Hunter: Forbidden Treasure screenshots](/images/blog/0063/01_hxhkindan.png)

Hunter x Hunter: Kindan no Hihou (translated roughly as Hunter x Hunter: The Forbidden Treasure) is an action-adventure role-playing video game released for the handheld console Game Boy Color in 2001. It's one of 8 HxH games released in 2000/2001, and one of two GBC games in the franchise (the other one being the infamous Hunter no Keifu/Hunter's Genealogy, a game considered to be a "legendary kusoge/crappy game" in Japan).

So, what is this game? It's an action RPG of sorts, though I guess nowadays it'd be classified as a "Metroidvania". It features a large, interconnected world, equipment to find/purchase, and multiple skills to learn (upon leveling up - max level being level 50), as well as 4 playable characters - Gon, Killua, Kurapika and Leorio, with their own skills/weapon types.

I've wanted to work on this game because it has an original (if rather simple) story that isn't based on any of the HxH arcs directly, and a neat ambience throughout; I'm also a big fan of the genre it belongs to, so one more retro game of this kind is quite nice in my book. It's also not a huge game, the last playthrough took me around 5-6 hours, with finding all of the treasures and doing most side stuff that this game offers. The translation is completed as far as the script goes, but there's still a good deal of work regarding the menus and such. Overall, it's a fun romp, but it requires the knowledge of Japanese to be able to know where to go/what to do at a given point in the story.

It's said that only 5% of the ocean has been explored in detail. Of the fish species we know of, scientists traditionally divide them into three categories: Agnatha (jawless), Chondrichthyes (cartilaginous), and Osteichthyes (bony). Evidently, none of them thought to boot up Space Fishermen, which has a detailed chart of (space) fish relations!

As there were no textless versions of the images, SONICMAN69 and Syko worked together to recreate the graphics from the ground up using the game's manual as a reference point. As you can see, the results are reel-y good!
