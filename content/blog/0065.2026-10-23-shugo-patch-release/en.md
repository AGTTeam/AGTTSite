## Shugo Chara! Rhythm Transformation♪ (しゅごキャラ！ ノリノリ！ キャラなりズム♪) patch by the Anime Game Translations Team

Released in 2009, Chara-nari Rhythm♪ is the last of three Japan-exclusive DS games based on the Shugo Chara license. A rhythm-action game, it features an original story set after episodes 85-86 of the anime. 

Players take on the role of “Cool & Spicy” heroine Amu Hinamori who has the power to purify X-Eggs - hearts which have been corrupted by fear and doubts. However, when a new type of X-Egg emerges, she must gather her strength and prepare to face her toughest challenge yet… taking singing lessons!
