## Death Note: Successors to L (デスノート Lを継ぐ者) patch by the Anime Game Translations Team

The second of three Death Note games for the Nintendo DS, Successors to L follows the series’ storyline in its entirety from Light finding the notebook to the Yellow Box Warehouse.

Get ready strategize, disrupt and use every trick in the book to outsmart the enemy across 11 story chapters, with gameplay inspired by the board game "Scotland Yard".

Practise your skills in Solo Mode, or go head-to-head against a friend in VS Mode, where you can choose from a wide roster of fan-favorite characters to serve as agents of justice.
